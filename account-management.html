Building Account Management for 1,100+ strategic customers

How JustCall's biggest customers went from being looked after one CSM at a time to having a function built around growing with them.

JustCall (SaaS Labs) · Lead Strategic Account Manager

At a glance

1,100+strategic customers in the portfolio
100–140%of quarterly targets on my own EMEA book
90EMEA accounts in my own book, alongside building the function

The problem

After building out scaled CS, I moved across to the other end of the base. Our strategic customers were some of the most valuable we had, but growing with them depended on individual effort rather than a shared way of working, so expansion was hard to plan and even harder to forecast.

Setting up the function

I partnered with CS leadership to design a repeatable expansion model for the strategic portfolio, and then co-built the forecasting and quota framework that went with it, so targets were set from real pipeline coverage rather than hope. I also owned renewals and expansion for my own EMEA book of 90 accounts, which kept the model honest because I was using it every day.

Making expansion repeatable

We ran multi-channel expansion and cross-sell campaigns for plan upgrades, annual conversions and new products like the AI Voice Agent, using product usage signals to decide who heard about what. For every feature launch I put together GTM collateral and hosted live webinars, so the team had an upsell story ready from day one.

What came of it

My EMEA book consistently landed at 100 to 140% of quarterly targets, and strategic account feedback started reaching Product, Marketing and the CEO's office as part of how decisions got made.

A project inside the role

Nexford: an AI Voice Agent that qualifies leads

The problem. Only about 10% of Nexford's inbound leads were ready for a sales rep, and they had no way to pre-vet them. Thousands of calls a month were going unanswered, and planned marketing in new regions was about to add more.

What I decided. I recommended JustCall's AI Voice Agent to handle inbound calls and pass only qualified leads to sales, rather than adding people to answer phones.

How we built it. The agent had to ask around ten qualifying questions in a warm, conversational voice, handle courses and prices that change by country, offer scholarships only to the right learners, answer website questions without making things up, and tag every answer in HubSpot for the sales team. Getting that reliable took several iterations and two months of testing.

The result. In testing, the agent sorted more than 85% of leads correctly, and Nexford went on to buy an AI Voice Agent license.
